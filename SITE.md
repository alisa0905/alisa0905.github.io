# Alisa Bakhareva: Portfolio

> Brand designer in Dubai: brand identity, social media, UI/UX and print. Lives at **alisabakhareva.me**.

## Brand Identity
- **Personality:** playful, colourful, confident, hand-made. Taken straight from Alisa's Figma/PDF portfolio.
- **Colours:** sky `#7cb9e4`, blue `#3883de`, green `#518c2d`, red `#b83244`, orange `#de7315`, pink `#e173ae`, mustard `#deba38`, sunny yellow `#f3c11b`, cream `#ecedd8`, ink `#1e1e1e`, paper background `#f6f3ea`.
- **Fonts:** Londrina Solid (big titles), Figtree (UI and labels), Author (longer body text).
- **Signature details:** wavy blobs, five-petal flowers that spin, curved titles. All of them are real vector art exported from the Figma file.

## Pages
- **Home** (`/`): orange hero with a huge two-line "Hi! I'm / Alisa." headline (letters swell when the mouse moves near them, on computers only), two buttons, the pink blob (her photo and the spinning blue flower are hidden for now), and 10 colourful squishy flowers in her flower shape: on computers they drop in on the right, squish (the whole flower) when they land or bump, and drift away from the mouse; on phones they sit as a pile at the bottom that hops when tapped, jumps when the phone is shaken and rolls when it is tilted (a hidden extra, no hint on the page). Flowers never overlap. Then a skills ribbon, Selected work (Cloud Castle first, full width), "Who am I?" on sky blue with dark text (bio, experience, publications with the IEEE paper link, education, toolbox) and extra space at the bottom, so the footer's hill rises out of the same blue.
- **Work** (`/work`): all 17 projects with filter chips: Jabrni, Freelance, Amana Homes, Quotify, Personal & Uni.
- **Case studies** (`/work/[project]`): one page per project, with a coloured header, client/year/role/tools, cover image, story text, image galleries, before/after comparisons and a "Next project" link. Cloud Castle includes website screenshots and a button linking to the live site, cloudcastle.co.
- **CV** (`/cv`): Alisa's CV, built from the Figma CV design. On screen it looks like the Figma page, and every company and project name links out. The "Download PDF" button gives a one-page A4 PDF laid out so hiring software (ATS) can read it cleanly.
- **Contact** (`/contact`): enquiry form (name, email, company, services, timeline, message). Sending opens the visitor's email app, addressed to alisabakhareva2902@gmail.com. No account needed.

## Components
- **Header** (`components/SiteHeader.tsx`): floating pill with Alisa's full name, Home, About, Work, CV (in that order) and a "Let's talk" button (opens Alisa's Calendly to book a call). Turns solid when you scroll. A soft highlight pill slides to the current page; on the home page it slides between Home and About as you scroll, and clicking Home or About there glides the page instead of jumping. Has a menu on phones.
- **Footer** (`components/SiteFooter.tsx`): matches Alisa's Figma footer (Things file, frame 989:1229): blue sky, a green hill, four big filled flowers (pink, blue, yellow and orange, two hanging off the edges), a curved "Let's Talk →" (opens Calendly; its letters pop in one by one and swell near the cursor, exactly like "Hi! I'm Alisa.", see `components/TalkArc.tsx`), email, "Based in Dubai, UAE", links and copyright. It's a **sticky footer**: it stays pinned at the bottom of the screen and the page slides up off it (`components/StickyReveal.tsx`). On computers everything is sized from the design so it always fits on one screen.
- **Project card** (`components/ProjectCard.tsx`): image tile, category chip, title and summary, with hover animation.
- **Case study layout** (`components/CaseBlocks.tsx`): turns each project's content list into text sections, image grids and before/after blocks.
- **Extras:** click any case-study image to zoom it full screen (`Lightbox`). Smooth scrolling (`SmoothScroll`). A flower cursor on desktop (`Cursor`). Scroll-in animations (`Reveal` plus the CSS in `app/globals.css`).

## How to Customize
- **Edit the CV:** all the CV text is in `lib/cv.ts`; section colours are set in `app/cv/page.tsx` (the `accent` on each section) (wrap words in `**double stars**` to make them bold; write `[Cloud Castle:](https://cloudcastle.co/)` for a bold underlined link). After editing, ask Claude to "regenerate the CV PDF" so the download matches — the PDF lives at `public/cv/Alisa-Bakhareva-CV.pdf`.
- **Edit, add or reorder projects:** everything lives in `lib/projects.ts`. Each project has a title, client, category, year, role, tools, colours, summary, cover image and a list of content blocks. Set `featured: true` to show it on the home page. The order on the home page is set at the top of `app/page.tsx`.
- **Add images:** put them in `public/work/` and add their size to `lib/assets.ts`.
- **Change the contact email, LinkedIn or booking link:** `CONTACT_EMAIL`, `LINKEDIN_URL` and `BOOKING_URL` (the Calendly link every "Let's talk" button uses) at the bottom of `lib/projects.ts` (the CV uses the same LinkedIn link).
- **Change colours:** the palette is at the top of `app/globals.css`.
